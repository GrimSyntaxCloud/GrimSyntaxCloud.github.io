FOOTAGE AND EDIT
Fresh footage captured October 14, 2026 from the live version 30 game at
https://trivia-turbo-race.grassyape5.chatgpt.site/ using two browser player sessions
controlled for demonstration (Turbo Ace and Neon Rival).
Tracks shown: Cosmic Speedway and Neon City. The Neon City finish and podium
come from the same recorded race as the answer/boost/steering sequence.
Gameplay is cropped, rearranged, and briefly held on the question for readability.
No results or game UI were fabricated. Promotional typography and animated
presentation panels were added in editing. Source frames are timestamped,
with approximately 15–18 frames/second during the continuous action takes.
The 30 fps export interpolates no gameplay frames; motion graphics render at 30 fps.
Music and game effects were mixed in editing, not recorded from browser audio.
